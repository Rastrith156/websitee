About Dayananda Sagar University

Dayananda Sagar Institutions, established in 1960, has grown into one of India's leading educational institutions. With over five decades of experience in academic excellence, the institution is committed to advancing knowledge and educating students in a way that makes them globally competitive. DSU has become a preferred choice for students seeking quality education combined with industry-ready skills.
