Dayananda Sagar Institutions, established in 1960, has grown into one of India's leading educational institutions. With over five decades of experience in academic excellence, the institution is committed to advancing knowledge and educating students in a way that makes them globally competitive. DSU has become a preferred choice for students seeking quality education combined with industry-ready skills.

Admissions 2025-26

Join Dayananda Sagar University

We welcome applications from qualified students worldwide. Our holistic admissions process considers academic achievements, test scores, and personal qualities. DSU offers opportunities through multiple admission channels including DSAT, PGCET, CET, and direct admissions.

Admission Requirements:

  • ✓ High school diploma or equivalent
  • ✓ Standardized test scores (SAT/DSAT/PGCET/CET)
  • ✓ Minimum required GPA/Merit
  • ✓ Application essay
  • ✓ Letters of recommendation

Entrance Exams & Codes:

🎯 CET Code (B.Tech): E240 | 🎯 Comed-K Code: E182
🎯 PGCET (M.Tech): T970 | 🎯 PGCET (MBA): B365MB
🎯 PGCET (MCA): C520MC
